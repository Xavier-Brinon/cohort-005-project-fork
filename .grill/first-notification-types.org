Facts from the code:
- Refund Requests have three states in code: =open=, =approved=, =declined=. The
  glossary's Pending, Issued and Failed Refund have no code yet, so nothing can
  trigger "Refund issued" or "Refund failed" today.
- The Instructor's question queue (=instructor.questions.tsx=) is built from
  top-level comments on lessons. Replies are one level deep.
- A Platform Admin's decline requires a note to the buyer; an approval's note is
  optional.
- Seats are claimed through =redeem.$code.tsx= (the =coupons= table).

* Round 1

Pick a small first set (about four types) that covers at least two roles, chosen
by one test: /someone is waiting on another person's action/. Everything else
goes to a "later types" list on the map's fog.

*** Recommendation
Yes: about four types, waiting-on-someone test, the rest listed as later.

* Round 2
Facts behind these questions, from the code:
- Cadence has exactly one kind of comment: the =comments= table, posted under a
  lesson by anyone who can see it. Course ratings are stars only, no text.
- "Question" is already taken: a Quiz has =quiz_questions=.
- The Instructor's "Unanswered Questions" queue treats *every* top-level comment
  as a question. It counts as answered once the course's Instructor or a
  Platform Admin has a live (not deleted) reply under it.

** DONE Q11 Lesson Comment as the umbrella term
- *Lesson Comment* :: a message someone posts under a lesson, visible to
  everyone on that lesson. It is either a Lesson Question or a Lesson Reply.
  /Avoid/: Comment (on its own), Post, Message.
Accept?

*** Recommendation
Yes. The "Lesson" prefix rules out confusion with a future comment elsewhere
(e.g. on a course), and the rule "either a Lesson Question or a Lesson Reply"
says there is no third kind.

*** Answer
OK

** DONE Q12 Lesson Question: is every top-level Lesson Comment one?
- *Lesson Question* :: a Lesson Comment that starts a thread under a lesson. It
  waits in the Instructor's queue until the course's Instructor or a Platform
  Admin replies. /Avoid/: Question (on its own, collides with Quiz Question),
  Thread, Top-level comment.
This matches the code: every top-level Lesson Comment is a Lesson Question, even
"Great lesson!". The alternative (only some are questions, marked by the author)
would be new product work. Accept the code's meaning?

*** Recommendation
Yes, every thread opener is a Lesson Question. Marking is out of scope.

*** Answer
OK

** DONE Q13 Lesson Reply and Answered
- *Lesson Reply* :: a Lesson Comment posted under a Lesson Question. Replies go
  one level deep: nobody replies to a Lesson Reply. /Avoid/: Answer, Response,
  Child comment.
- *Answered Lesson Question* :: a Lesson Question with a live Lesson Reply from
  the course's Instructor or a Platform Admin. A Student's Lesson Reply does not
  answer it.
Accept both?

*** Recommendation
Yes. With these, the first-set types read: "new Lesson Question, to the
Instructor" and "new Lesson Reply, to the author of the Lesson Question".

*** Answer
OK

** DONE Q14 Quiz Question
Add *Quiz Question* :: one question in a lesson's Quiz. /Avoid/: Question (on
its own). It isn't a Notification term, but it makes Lesson Question
unambiguous. Accept?

*** Recommendation
Yes, add it with the others.

*** Answer
yes file:../GLOSSARY.md

* Summary
- Q1 :: First set of four types, chosen by "someone is waiting on another
  person's action".
- Q2 :: Standing rule: the person who acted is never notified about their own
  action.
- Q3 :: Ships: Refund Request decided (approved or declined, with decision
  note), to the buyer.
- Q4 :: Ships: new Open Refund Request, to every Platform Admin; each copy stays
  until read.
- Q5 :: Later: Refund issued / Refund failed, waiting on the money side of
  Refunds; must stay tracked.
- Q6 :: Ships: new Lesson Question, to the course's Instructor.
- Q7 :: Ships: new Lesson Reply, to the author of the Lesson Question only.
- Q8 :: Later: Seat claimed, to the Team Admin.
- Q9 :: Edits and deletions never create a Notification.
- Q10-Q14 :: Glossary: Lesson Comment, Lesson Question, Lesson Reply, Answered
  Lesson Question, Quiz Question.
