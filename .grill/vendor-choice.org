Where the shortlist stands:
- All three (Knock, Novu, SuprSend) pass every hard criterion. None documents
  React Router 7; the inbox is client-only everywhere and the prototype must
  prove it.
- Knock :: local RS256 token, unlimited environments on Free, richest status
  model, $250/mo next tier, no EU residency yet, closed source.
- Novu :: HMAC token, MIT open core, US + EU residency on Free, $30/mo next
  tier; Free caps at 5 workflows and 2 environments (Dev, Prod); real-time
  transport is inferred from a =socketUrl=, not stated.
- SuprSend :: local ES256 token with refresh, Staging + Production on Free,
  $100/mo next tier; SSR undocumented, pricing page possibly stale.
- The first set has four Notification types, so on Novu's Free tier one workflow
  slot is left.

* Round 1
** DONE Q1 Does EU data residency matter?
Cadence records the buyer's country (ADR 0009) and sells to students anywhere.
Notifications will carry personal data (e.g. a Platform Admin's decision note on
a Refund Request). Knock has no EU residency yet; Novu has it on Free.
- (a) Required: rules Knock out.
- (b) A tie-breaker: preferred, not required.
- (c) Ignore it for now.

*** Recommendation
(b) A tie-breaker. Nothing in Cadence today stores data by region, so making it
a hard rule would be new policy, not a notification decision.

*** Answer
b

** DONE Q2 How much does the cost of growing matter?
The four first-set types fit every Free tier. The fifth and later types (Refund
issued/failed, Seat claimed, thread activity) push Novu past its 5-workflow cap.
Next tiers: Novu $30/mo, SuprSend $100/mo, Knock $250/mo. Weigh "cheap to grow"
as a strong criterion?

*** Recommendation
Yes, strong. The later types are already tracked, so growth is planned, not
hypothetical.

*** Answer
Not really actually

** DONE Q3 Is two environments enough for QA mode?
QA mode (in the map's fog) wants testing kept away from real users' inboxes.
Novu Free gives Dev and Prod: local development and QA would share Dev. Knock
gives unlimited environments; SuprSend gives Staging + Production. Is "Dev and
Prod only" acceptable?

*** Recommendation
Yes. One non-production environment keeps tests away from real users, which is
the point of QA mode; separating local dev from QA is a refinement.

*** Answer
Agreed

** DONE Q4 How much weight does open source carry?
The map lists open source / self-hostable as nice to have. Only Novu has an open
core (MIT), and even there self-hosting is sold on Enterprise. Keep it as a
minor tie-breaker?

*** Recommendation
Yes, minor: it lowers lock-in (you could read or fork the SDK), but nobody plans
to self-host.

*** Answer
ok

** DONE Q5 Widen the field?
The research skipped Engagespot and NotificationAPI. Evaluate them before
deciding?

*** Recommendation
No. Three vendors already pass every hard criterion; another round of research
would not change the shape of the choice.

*** Answer
Actually yes try them too

* Round 2
New facts since Round 1:
- [[https://github.com/Xavier-Brinon/cohort-005-project-fork/issues/9][Do Engagespot or NotificationAPI beat the vendor shortlist?]]: no. Both fail
  product health (Engagespot's site is dead and its SDKs are stale;
  NotificationAPI, now Pingram, has moved away from in-app).
- Shortlist health on the npm registry (2026-11-14): =@knocklabs/react= 0.15.1
  and =@knocklabs/node= 1.41.1 published in November 2026; =@suprsend/react=
  1.5.0 in October; =@novu/react= 3.19.2 in September. All three are alive.

** DONE Q6 Adopt Knock?
With your Round 1 weights (cost barely matters, EU and open source are only
tie-breakers), Knock leads on what's left:
- unlimited environments on Free, so QA mode can have its own environment apart
  from local development;
- user token signed locally (RS256) with an expiry, no network call in the
  loader;
- the richest read state (seen, read, interacted, archived), which the
  read/unread fog will need;
- the most recently published SDKs and a documented client-only component.
Accepted costs: no EU residency yet (promised by end of 2026), closed source,
$250/mo next tier.

*** Recommendation
Yes, adopt Knock.

*** Answer
Agreed

** DONE Q7 Runner-up if the prototype fails
The prototype must still prove the React Router 7 pattern (token signed in a
loader, inbox mounted after hydration) and real-time delivery. If Knock fails
that, fall back to Novu without reopening this grill?

*** Recommendation
Yes: Novu is the named fallback; a failure only reopens the choice if Novu fails
too.

*** Answer
Agreed

** DONE Q8 Record it as an ADR?
The choice is hard to reverse (feeds, read state and user tokens live with the
vendor), surprising without context (a reader may ask why not the open-source,
EU-ready, cheaper Novu), and the result of a real trade-off. Write
=docs/adr/0013-knock-for-in-app-notifications.md=?

*** Recommendation
Yes, if Q6 is yes. It names the trade-offs from Q1 to Q4 and the Novu fallback.

*** Answer
Yes
