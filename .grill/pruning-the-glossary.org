** DONE Q2 What is the glossary's scope
The header says it covers "buying courses and getting money back after a
purchase". The last seed added terms beyond that: ~Instructor~ (a role with no
part in refunds), ~Lapsed~ (an instructor-roster badge), ~Watched~.

Options:
- (a) Purchases and Refunds only: drop everything outside, including the terms
  the refund rules lean on.
- (b) Purchases, Refunds, and a learner's place on a course: keep what refund
  language needs (Learner, Watched, Watch Limit, place on a course, Lapsed),
  drop ~Instructor~.
- (c) The whole platform: keep everything, widen the header.

*** Recommendation
(b). Approving a Refund Request ends a learner's place on a course, and the
Watch Limit decides whether a Refund Request may be raised, so those words are
needed. ~Instructor~ plays no part in buying or refunding.

*** Answer
Agreed

Header widened in [[file:../GLOSSARY.md][GLOSSARY.md]]; ~Instructor~ removed.

** DONE Q3 Rewrite Watch Limit as a Refund Request rule
Current entry: "A Refund is only offered on a course with under two hours
watched. Past that, the Learner has had the course, and the refund form turns
them away."

Three problems:
- It is a rule with a number in it. The number has already drifted: the code
  (=app/lib/refund-rules.ts=, ~REFUND_WATCH_LIMIT_SECONDS = 60 * 60~) says one
  hour, the glossary says two.
- The limit gates raising a Refund Request, not the Refund (money). Elsewhere
  the glossary keeps those apart.
- "the refund form turns them away" is UI behaviour.

Proposed entry:

: Watch Limit: The amount of Watched time on a course past which the buyer
: can no longer raise a Refund Request for it.
: Avoid: Watch cap, Viewing threshold

*** Recommendation
Yes, use the proposed entry. The number lives in code only.

*** Answer
agreed

Applied in [[file:../GLOSSARY.md][GLOSSARY.md]].

** DONE Q6 Enrollment or "place on a course"
Two words for one thing:
- The ADRs (0001, 0002, 0004) and Approved Refund Request say "a learner's place
  on a course".
- ~Lapsed~ and ~Instructor~ say "Enrollment" / "enrolled"; the schema table is
  ~enrollments~.

Pick one canonical term, define it, and list the other under Avoid.

course, however they got it." Avoid: place on a course, Access. Reword Approved
Refund Request to say Enrollment. Leave the ADRs as written; they are a
historical record.

* Round 2
** DONE Q7 What does Lapsed mean
The glossary says: "An Enrollment that is no longer active." The code disagrees.
=app/lib/enrollments.ts= has ~isLapsed~ return true when ~enrollmentId ===
null~: a Lapsed learner has /no/ Enrollment at all, not an inactive one. The
roster finds them as learners with lesson progress on the course and no
Enrollment (=app/routes/instructor.$courseId.students.tsx=).

With Enrollment now defined as "a Learner's place on a course", "inactive
Enrollment" names something that does not exist.

Proposed entry:

: Lapsed: A Learner who has Watched part of a course but no longer has an
: Enrollment on it, for example after an Approved Refund Request.
: Avoid: Expired, Inactive, Revoked

The current entry also mentions "the instructor's student roster", which is UI
and uses two words we avoid or dropped (Instructor, student).

*** Recommendation
Yes, use the proposed entry.

*** Answer
agreed

Applied in [[file:../GLOSSARY.md][GLOSSARY.md]].

* Round 3
** DONE Q11 Is a Team Admin a Learner
Learner is now "someone who watches courses on Cadence, through an Individual
Purchase or a Claimed Seat". Three entries still use lowercase "learner":
- Team Admin: "A learner who buys and manages a Team Licence."
- Individual Purchase: "A Purchase a learner makes for themselves."
- Seat, Unclaimed Seat, Claimed Seat: "a learner can claim", "no learner has
  claimed".

Individual Purchase and the Seat entries mean Learner, so capitalising them is
safe. Team Admin is different: a Team Licence "grants Seats, not the course
itself; the buyer can't watch the course through it". A Team Admin who never
claims a Seat watches nothing, so calling them a Learner contradicts both
entries.

Proposed Team Admin entry:

: Team Admin: Someone who buys and manages a Team Licence. A Team Admin is not
: a Learner through it, and has no say over Refund Requests.
: Avoid: Admin (on its own)

*** Recommendation
Yes: reword Team Admin as proposed, and capitalise "learner" to Learner in
Individual Purchase and the three Seat entries.

*** Answer
agreed
